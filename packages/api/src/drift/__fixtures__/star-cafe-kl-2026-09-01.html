    FAQ

    Helpful answers before you visit

    Is Star Cafe open on Monday?

    Yes, Star Cafe is open daily from 12pm to 12am. Hot kitchen is closed on Mondays, however we serve bagels freshly made in-house. Usual kitchen hours are 12pm to 9.30pm, on other days.

    Does Star Cafe take bookings?

    Yes. You can book a table through WhatsApp or Google Calendar on the Star Cafe website. For bookings of more than 8 people, please book via WhatsApp.

    What food does Star Cafe serve?

    Star Cafe serves cafe favourites, comfort food, sharing plates, mains, coffee, cocktails, beer, and other drinks. You can explore the food and drinks menu on the website.

    Where is Star Cafe located?

    Star Cafe is located at First Floor, 65, Jalan 109E, Taman Desa, Off Jalan Klang Lama, 58100 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

    Is Star Cafe, Taman Desa a work-friendly cafe?

    Yes, we're a work-friendly cafe with fast WiFi, comfortable seating, relaxed atmosphere, power-plugs and indoor & outdoor seatings. Making Star Cafe, Taman Desa a popular choice for remote workers looking for all of these in one place.

    Is there parking at Star Cafe Taman Desa?

    Yes. Star Cafe Taman Desa has free and easy parking available.

    Is there set lunch at Star Cafe Taman Desa?

    Yes, we have set lunch available from 12pm to 4pm every Tuesday to Sunday. That's right, including weekends!

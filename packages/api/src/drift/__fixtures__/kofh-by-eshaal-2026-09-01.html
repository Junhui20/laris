01

Small beginnings.
Still in every cup.

Kofh began at the roadside, with a coffee machine at the back of a car and a simple idea: make every cup feel personal.

What started with neighbours, late-night drivers, and familiar faces has grown into a café made for coffee, food, and good conversations.

The address changed. The heart behind it did not.

# Contact and Booking replace "lead"

"Lead" was doing two incompatible jobs: in one place it meant an observed act of interest, in another an actual piece of business won. The two are not interchangeable, and the gap between them is unobservable to us by design — once a customer taps through to WhatsApp we deliberately stop watching, because we do not automate the merchant's account.

So the term is retired and split. **Contact** is dense, automatic and same-day (a WhatsApp tap, a call, a directions tap, an availability check) and is the dependent variable for anything statistical, because statistics need sample size. **Booking** is sparse, merchant-reported and real money, and carries the return-on-investment story.

## Consequences

The merchant has one recurring manual input: a periodic count of direct bookings. This is deliberate. It is a number they already know, it is the number they actually care about, and inferring it instead would mean either automating their WhatsApp — which we have ruled out — or quietly estimating revenue, which would make every ROI claim we produce unfalsifiable.
